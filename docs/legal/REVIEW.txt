EULA REVIEW

The owner-completed agreement identifies Stephen Lee, licensing contact,
effective date, Washington governing law, and court venue. It permits personal
and internal business use, including local modifications, while restricting
redistribution, code incorporation into other products, and hosted services.
Those permitted uses are intentional; this is not an evaluation-only license.

GitHub platform rights, mandatory legal rights, third-party permissions, and
users' ownership of their own projects are preserved. Public visibility is not
represented as an open-source grant. Terms do not claim that a license can
prevent copying, prevent independent competing development, or revoke platform
rights. Warranty, liability, termination, and non-retroactive change provisions
are present; mandatory-law exceptions remain. Enforceability depends on the
facts and applicable law and is not established by repository checks.

ACCEPTANCE AND PACKAGING EVIDENCE

scripts/build-release.mjs generates a packaged entry point that displays the
exact bundled EULA with repopilot --license without acceptance. Other commands
require REPOPILOT_ACCEPT_EULA=1 before importing the operational CLI. Missing
acceptance returns a nonzero result and is smoke-tested not to create repository
state. The optional completion installer skips shell setup without acceptance.

Acceptance is an explicit environment assertion for EULA v1.0, not a signature,
identity verification, centrally stored record, or tamper-proof DRM. The public
source and internal modules can be invoked outside the packaged entry point;
the EULA and source-install instructions require acceptance for source builds,
but source builds do not have the packaged runtime gate. No claim is made that
technical controls prevent copying or that the gate proves informed assent.

README.md and docs/releases/v0.3.0.md explain review and acceptance. Package
metadata points to the actual EULA, and packaging checks verify that the
installed EULA and third-party notice file match their reviewed source copies.

This inventory covers the shipped standalone CLI JavaScript. It is not a license

providers, user repositories, or future native/platform artifacts. Review those
separately if distributed. Toolchain packages installed locally are not bundled
merely because they appear in the workspace lockfile.
